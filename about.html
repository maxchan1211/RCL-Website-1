Who we are

Newly incorporated, backed by experienced hands

RCL Engineering & Facilities Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysia-based engineering and facilities solutions provider incorporated in 2025, specialising in mechanical and electrical (M&E) engineering and integrated facilities management, together with specialist design and engineering services.

RCL is majority Bumiputera-owned, with 80% Bumiputera ownership, and operates with a dedicated professional and technical team covering engineering, project delivery and specialist design disciplines.

Although RCL is a newly incorporated company, its leadership and engineering team bring relevant industry experience to the organisation, combining the agility of a new company with the technical knowledge and practical experience of its people.
